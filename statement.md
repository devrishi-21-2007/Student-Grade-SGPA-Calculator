# Student Grade & SGPA Calculator

## Problem Statement

Calculating grades, total marks, credit points, and SGPA manually can take time and may lead to calculation errors. This project provides a simple Python-based solution that automatically processes student marks and generates the required academic results.


## Scope of the Project

The project is designed to calculate the grades and SGPA of students for multiple subjects based on their marks and credit values. It can be further improved by adding more subjects, student records, semester-wise results, and data storage.


## Target Users

1. College and university students

2. Teachers and faculty members

3. Beginners learning Python

4. Anyone who wants to calculate grades and SGPA quickly


## High-Level Features

1. Student information input

2. Subject-wise marks and grade calculation

3. Automatic grade point calculation

4. Absent and invalid input handling

5. Total and average marks calculation

6. Credit point calculation

7. Automatic SGPA calculation

8. Clear and organized result display
